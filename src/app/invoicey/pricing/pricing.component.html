Pricing & License FAQ

Are there any setup fees or hidden charges?

No. There are absolutely no onboarding fees, configuration fees, or hidden charges. The price listed is final and includes standard software updates.

Can I cancel or change my plan anytime?

Yes. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your active subscription plan at any time directly through your account dashboard. Upgrades apply immediately.

Do I need a GSTIN to register or use Invoicey?

Yes, you need a valid GSTIN (Goods and Services Tax Identification Number) to register on Invoicey. This ensures that all generated invoices, tax calculations (CGST/SGST/IGST), and exportable financial reports remain fully compliant with Indian tax regulations.

What happens after my 30-day free trial ends?

At the end of your 30-day evaluation period, you will be prompted to choose a Premium Monthly, 6-Month, or Yearly plan to continue generating invoices. Your existing catalogs and data will remain safe.
